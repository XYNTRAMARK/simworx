How we build your simulator

A proven process with customer involvement where it matters.

1

Research

We study the aircraft and mission, visit reference aircraft or museums where possible, obtain drawings and establish the engineering baseline.

2

CAD design

We design shells, interiors, panels, controls, avionics integration, electronics and service access in CAD.

3

Prototyping & development

We build and test prototypes to bring unique systems to life and validate performance.

4

Procurement

We source components, manage external suppliers and confirm compatibility across the whole platform.

5

Assembly & integration

Hardware, electronics, controls, visuals and software are assembled and integrated into one system.

6

Customer factory assessment

You review the build while there is still time to incorporate practical feedback and make the final development push.

7

Factory sign-off

We test the finished simulator against the original project criteria and complete the full flight and functional assessment.

8

Breakdown & shipping

The simulator is carefully dismantled, protected, crated and prepared for transport.

9

Installation

Our team reassembles, integrates and verifies the simulator at the customer location.

10

Training & support

Client training, acceptance testing and ongoing support complete the programme.
