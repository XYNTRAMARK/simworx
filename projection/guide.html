Find a layout for your room

  1. Room & budget. Enter internal dimensions and clear height below beams. Add an optional complete equipment budget.
  2. Screen. Choose the enclosure, surface, diameter, arc and height. Projectors stay hidden.
  3. Cockpit. Choose your simulator and check its position and pilot eyepoints. Supplied sizes are preset; custom sizes stay in a closed section.
  4. Find projectors. Ask for a matched set or select an existing model. The planner searches one-height ceiling layouts within the room and documented lens restrictions, then checks cockpit shadows.
  5. Review. Inspect the pilot-visible coverage, remaining gaps and mounting proposal. Save the configuration or print the lens-centre mounting plan.

Green means a good sampled geometric fit. Amber shows compromises; red means significant gaps or a layout that could not be found. Unknown prices never establish that a proposal is within budget.

Advanced individual positioning remains available. Recalculate after changing the room, screen, cockpit or projector choice. Focus, brightness, mounting datums and the final installation require review.
