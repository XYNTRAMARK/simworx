SIMWORX SIGN
PDF signing — browser-only first release

Prepare your signed PDFs

Select a PDF, position your electronic mark and save a new signed copy. PDF contents remain in your browser and are not uploaded by this page.

Current limitation: This utility draws a visible electronic mark onto a PDF and records a local hash. It does not authenticate signers, send secure invitations, create a cryptographic PDF signature, or provide a verified third-party audit trail. Do not treat it as a complete two-party signing workflow.
