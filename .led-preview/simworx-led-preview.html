      TECHNICAL INFORMATION

      Plan the installation with confidence.

      The final technical quotation identifies the selected hardware, performance, market documentation and installation requirements.

      Expected operating life

      Rated operating life confirmed with the selected supplier specification.

      Compliance by market

      Europe / EEA

      Supplier conformity documentation to be confirmed for the selected hardware and installation.

      United States / North America

      Applicable market-specific certification or listing information to be confirmed. European conformity does not establish North American certification.

      Manufacturing time

      Curved, hemispherical and custom-system lead times confirmed with quotation.

      Pixel pitch & viewing distance

      Pixel pitch is the distance between LED pixels. Smaller pitch produces a finer image at shorter viewing distances. The correct choice also depends on the actual pilot/driver position, image content and visual setup. The curvature radius is a geometry reference, not a guaranteed eye-to-screen distance.

      Refresh rate & brightness

      Refresh-rate specification confirmed for the selected hardware.

      Brightness specification confirmed with quotation. Simulator visual systems normally operate in controlled indoor lighting.

      Power requirements

      Power requirements provided with final technical quotation.

      Serviceability

      Module access, spare compatibility and required service clearances confirmed for the selected display and frame.

      Environmental requirements

      Indoor environmental limits, ventilation and thermal requirements confirmed with the supplier specification. Room conditions should be included in custom requirements.

      Warranty

      Standard LED-system warranty duration and coverage are confirmed in the formal quotation. Extended cover can be requested; availability and terms require confirmation.

      Spare components

      Spare modules and compatible components allow repairs without waiting for international replacement supply. The final spares list depends on cabinet/module design, service access and the operating requirement.

      Installation & commissioning

      Request customer installation, remote support or Simworx onsite commissioning. The quotation confirms the suitable arrangement, site access, structural requirements and scope. Specialist spherical systems may require professional installation.

      Warp & calibration

      Warp and calibration align the generated imagery with the screen geometry and visual setup. A requested preconfigured file is a starting point, subject to compatible software and system details. Final site calibration and software licensing are confirmed in the quotation.

      Shipping

      Freight depends on screen size, packing/crating, destination, access requirements and the installation arrangement. Shipping confirmed with formal quotation.
