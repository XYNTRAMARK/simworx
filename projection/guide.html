Design the best visual system for your room

  1. Room & projector budget. Enter the internal dimensions and clear ceiling height, plus what you can spend on the projector set.
  2. Screen & cockpit together. Choose the enclosure and screen surface, size the curved screen, add your simulator and move it until the physical layout feels right. Projectors stay hidden while you do this.
  3. Projectors. Let the planner place a matched set automatically. It shows the best arrangement it can achieve, including coverage, black cockpit shadow and pilot-visible compromise. Choose an indicative projector class, test one of the examples, or import a projector you find online.
  4. Review & improve. Inspect the result from the pilot eyepoint, test moving the cockpit or changing the screen, compare a shorter-throw projector and then save or print the mounting proposal.

The planner does not demand perfection. Green is a strong sampled geometric result; amber and red quantify compromises that remain visible so you can decide what matters. If a known projector is over budget, the closest practical layout can still be shown rather than hidden.

The “Projector specification this geometry wants” panel gives an indicative target throw ratio and mounting geometry. Advanced individual positioning remains available, but the automatic layout is the normal starting point. Focus, brightness, mounting datums and final installation still require review.
